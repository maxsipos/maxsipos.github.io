MAXsi POS

MAXsi POS is an Android-based Point of Sale (POS) application designed to help small and medium retail businesses manage daily operations efficiently. It provides a simple way to record sales, manage inventory, and review business performance through clear reports.

Category: Point of Sale, Retail Management
Platform: Android
Target Users: Retail SMEs, grocery stores, minimarkets
License: MIT License
Official Website Android Project Page GitHub Repository

Overview

MAXsi POS focuses on practical retail workflows and ease of use. It is suitable for businesses that need a lightweight POS system without complex configuration or enterprise-level overhead.

The application is designed primarily for Indonesian SMEs but is also suitable for retail businesses in other growing markets.

Key Features

  • Cash and cashless sales transactions
  • Product and inventory management
  • Stock monitoring and control
  • Sales reports and profit & loss summaries
  • Daily and monthly transaction recaps
  • Transaction history tracking
  • Simple and user-friendly interface
  • Optimized for small and medium retail businesses
